Lorenzo Caputi

I'm an MS student in Statistics at Stanford and a graduate researcher at MIT with Prof. Dimitris Bertsimas. Before this, I studied Computer Science and Economics at Bocconi and wrote my thesis at Harvard with Prof. Tomer Ullman. I also co-founded BAINSA, Italy's largest student-run AI research lab.

In my research, I work on what I think is the most important scientific problem of our time: building intelligent systems that create new scientific knowledge, autonomously and safely. More specifically, I am interested in these research questions:

  1. How can we teach a language model "research taste" using signal from its own exploration?
  2. How can we best use test-time compute to efficiently explore the space of text-based solutions to open scientific problems? For example, through evolutionary search or test-time learning.
  3. How can we reliably use these methods to solve high-impact open problems in science and industrial R&D?

If you are interested in these topics, feel free to email me for a chat or a potential collaboration.
