Lorenzo Caputi

I'm an MS student in Statistics at Stanford and a graduate researcher at MIT with Prof. Dimitris Bertsimas. I work on autonomous research: AI systems that create new scientific knowledge. I study whether RL post-training can teach them research taste, and how to best use test-time compute to explore open problems. Before this, I studied Computer Science and Economics at Bocconi and wrote my thesis at Harvard with Prof. Tomer Ullman. I also co-founded BAINSA, Italy's largest student-run AI research lab.
